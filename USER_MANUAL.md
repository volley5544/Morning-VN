# Morning FM — User Manual

**App:** Morning FM (Srisawad Corporation Plc.)
**Platform:** Mobile (Android / iOS)
**Audience:** Field staff, branch employees, collections officers, salespeople, and their supervisors/approvers.

Morning FM is the company's all-in-one field-operations and HR app. From one place, staff can clock in/out with GPS verification, request and approve leave, submit a resignation, work loan-collection accounts, follow up on sales leads, and track field employees' routes on a map.

> **Note on language:** The app runs in Thai and English. A language switch (Thai / English) is available from the login/menu area. Some button labels in this manual are shown in both languages because the live app mixes them.

## 1. Getting Started

### First-time sign in
1. **Login** — On the login screen, enter your **Username** and **Password**, then tap **LOGIN**.
2. **Set your PIN** — The first time you sign in, you'll be asked to create a PIN ("Set Your Pin"). Enter a PIN and tap **Confirm**. You'll use this PIN to open the app next time instead of retyping your password.
3. **Confirm Email** (if prompted) — Enter the email address where you want to receive information and tap **OK (ตกลง)**.

### Returning users
- **Unlock with PIN** — On reopening the app, enter your PIN on the "Enter Pin to Use App" screen. The app unlocks automatically once the correct PIN is complete.
- **Logout** — Tap **Logout** on the PIN screen to sign out and return to the login screen.
- **Clear Cache** — Tap **Clear Cache** on the PIN screen to reset cached data if the app is behaving unexpectedly.

### Change language
- Open the **Select Language** popup (from the login or menu area), choose **Thai** or **English** from the dropdown, and tap **Apply**.

## 2. Home & Navigation

The app has a few landing surfaces depending on your role:

### Super App hub (employee main menu)
The main hub for staff. It contains:
- **Header** — your profile photo and name, a **notification bell** (with an unread badge), and any expiry alerts (e.g. a broker card expiring soon).
- **Promotional banners** — swipeable news/promotion carousels.
- **Service menu** — tiles for the main features: **Check in-out**, **Leave Request**, **Collection**, and **Location employee** (some tiles show a badge with pending counts).
- **Tools menu** — additional utilities.
- **Side drawer** — app version and extra menu options.

> **Role-based menus:** The Service and Tools tiles you see depend on your role/permissions, so not every user sees every feature.

### Mobile Home (customer/lending dashboard)
A "super-app" style home with a bottom navigation bar:
- **Quick actions:** Top up credit line (เติมวงเงิน), Pay by QR (ชำระด้วย QR), Track status (ติดตามสถานะ).
- **My Loans (สินเชื่อของฉัน):** loan cards with due date and amount due, plus a **Pay (ชำระเงิน)** button.
- **News & Activities (ข่าวสารและกิจกรรม).**
- **Bottom tabs:** Home (หน้าแรก), Find Branch (ค้นหาสาขา), Notifications (แจ้งเตือน), More (เมนูอื่นๆ).

### Home / announcements page
A simple landing page with a **Promotion** carousel and an **Announcement** banner. Tap any image to open it.

## 3. Check-in / Attendance

Open the **Check-in** feature from the Service menu. It opens the **Check-in Dashboard** with three tiles: **Check in-out**, **Check-in history**, and **Change location**.

### Clock in / Clock out
1. From the dashboard, tap **Check in-out** (Thai: **เช็คอินเข้า - ออกงาน**).
2. The screen shows the **live date and time**.
3. **Take your selfie** — tap the photo area (camera icon) to capture your attendance photo.
4. **Select your branch/location** — the app detects your location; confirm or pick the correct branch from the dropdown. If you need a different branch, use **Search Branch** (type at least 3 characters, then tap a result).
5. **Add notes/reason** (หมายเหตุ) — optional; use this to explain an off-location check-in.
6. Tap **Record time (ลงเวลา) / Clock in-out** to submit.
7. A **success screen** confirms the check-in. Tap **Return to Dashboard** or **Check Status**.

> **GPS / geofencing:** Check-in matches your location to a branch. If you are outside the expected location, use the Notes/Reason field to explain.

### Check-in history
- Tap **Check-in history** to review your recorded times.
- Records are shown in a table — **Date, Clock in, Clock out, Status** — with tabs for the **current month** and **last month**.
- Status values include holiday (วันหยุด) and others; days with no time show "-".

> **Important:** Recorded times are forwarded to a separate "coach" system. Re-check your times the next day to confirm they were recorded correctly.

### Change location (supervisor/admin tool)
Used to correct a branch's stored GPS coordinates so check-in lands on the right spot.
1. Enter the **Branch code**, **new latitude**, and **new longitude**.
2. Tap **Search** to preview the **Old location** and **New location** on two maps, plus the **distance** between them.
3. Tap **Save** to commit the new coordinates.

*(Admins can also register a new branch via the "Add Branch" form: Branch Code, Latitude, Longitude, Branch Name, Area Code, Region Code.)*

## 4. Leave Management

Open **Leave Request** from the Service menu to reach the **Leave menu**, which has four tiles: **Leave list**, **Leave approval request list**, **Leave approval**, and **Approved leave list**.

### Check your leave balance
- **Leave list** shows each leave type with **All** (entitlement), **Used**, and **Remaining** counts.

### Submit a leave request
1. Open the **Submit approval request** form.
2. Choose the **Leave Type** (e.g. sick leave ลาป่วย, personal leave ลากิจ).
3. Choose **half-day or full-day** (ลาเต็มวัน) from the time-period dropdown.
4. **Pick your leave dates** on the calendar. Holidays are greyed out, and the app **auto-calculates the number of leave days**.
5. Enter your **contact phone number** and **reason for leave**.
6. Optionally **attach a document/image** (e.g. medical certificate) — a preview appears.
7. Tap **Save** to submit for approval.

### View & manage your requests
- **My Leave Requests (Approval request list)** — filter by **Year** and **Month**. Each card shows type, submission date, leave dates, number of days, period, reason, approver, status, and any attachment.
- Tap **View details (ดูรายละเอียด)**, **Edit** to change a request, or **Cancel** to cancel it.

### Edit a request
- The **Edit** form is the same as the submit form, pre-filled. Change any field and tap **Save**.

### Cancel a request
- On the **Cancel leave (ยกเลิกการลา)** confirmation, enter a **reason (ระบุเหตุผล)** and tap **OK (ตกลง)** to confirm, or **Cancel (ยกเลิก)** to back out.

### Approving leave (for approvers)
- **Leave approval queue** lists pending requests with employee nickname, department, leave details, reason, contact, and attachments.
- **Single action:** tap **Approve** or **Do not approve** (reject) on a card.
- **Batch approval:** use **Approve many people** → tick **Select all** or individual rows → tap **Approve selected**.
- **History:** the tabbed **Approval / history list** groups records into pending, approved, and cancelled, filterable by year and month.

## 5. Resignation

The **Submit resignation (ยื่นเรื่องขออนุมัติลาออก)** form is a short exit questionnaire:
1. **Last working day** — tap **Specify date (ระบุวันที่)** to set it.
2. **Reasons for resigning** — multi-select checklist (you can choose more than one) plus an **Other (อื่นๆ)** free-text field.
3. **Suggestions** for company improvement (free text).
4. **Contact details during the notice period** — Province / District / Sub-district / Postcode, plus address and phone.
5. **Willingness to return** — dropdown.
6. **Company assets to return** — multi-select checklist plus an **Other** field.
7. Tap **Save (บันทึก)** to submit.

## 6. Loan Collection

Open **Collection** from the Service menu. This module is for collections officers working overdue accounts.

### Pick a debt category
- The **Collection home** shows debt-type tiles, each with an account count so you can see workload per bucket. Tap a tile to open its account list.

### Work an account list
- The **Collection List** shows customers with vehicle registration and status.
- **Search**, **sort**, and **filter** the list; **pull down to refresh**. Tap a customer to open their detail.

### Customer / contract detail
- Shows every contract for the customer, with due date, overdue days/payments, status, outstanding balance, installments due, last-paid date, and number of recorded calls.
- **Tick one or more contracts**, then choose an action:
  - **Visit the area** → on-site collection (RP8 report).
  - **Record call results** → phone collection.
  - **Call history** or a **payment QR code** per contract.

### Record a phone-call result
1. On **Record Call Results**, review the read-only contract summary.
2. Pick a **Group ID** and a **call-result status** from dropdowns.
3. Set the **payment (promise) date**.
4. Either tick **"same amount for all contracts"** or enter a **payment amount** per contract *(required)*.
5. Add a **remark**, then tap **Save**. A success screen confirms.

### Record an on-site visit (RP8 report)
1. On **On-site / RP8 Remarks**, review the contract summary.
2. Select the **debt-collection result** from the dropdown.
3. **Capture/attach photos** as evidence.
4. Confirm the **auto-filled GPS coordinates** (also shown on an embedded map).
5. Type your **remarks**, then tap **Save**.

### Call history
- A read-only log of past collection records for a contract: call date-time, group code, call result, promise day, and remark.

## 7. Leads / Sales

Open the leads feature to reach the **Lead Dashboard**.

### Lead Dashboard
- Summary tiles grouped by **lead source** (self-found "หาเอง", telesales "เทเล", HO leads, all leads, asset types), each with a count.
- **Call-status breakdown:** All, Not yet called (ยังไม่ได้โทร), Reached (ติดต่อได้), Unreachable (ติดต่อไม่ได้), In contract (อยู่ระหว่างทำสัญญา).
- **Customer-source breakdown:** old closed accounts, hire-purchase, trucks, etc.
- Tap any tile to drill into the matching lead list.

### Lead List
- A working list of individual leads. **Search** by name, phone, branch code, or LeadID.
- Each lead card shows days elapsed, call status, and last-contact time.
- Per lead, tap **Record call (บันทึกการโทร)** to log an outcome or **Call history (ประวัติการโทร)** to review past calls.

## 8. Employee Tracking

Open **Location employee** from the Service menu to see where field staff traveled.

1. **Search Employee** — type an employee ID or name (at least 3 characters), tap **Search**, and select an employee.
2. **Pick a date** — on the employee's tracking screen, confirm their profile (ID, name, position, branch) and choose the **location date**, then tap **check Location**.
3. **View the route** — the **Tracking** screen shows:
   - Employee header with the total number of recorded locations.
   - A side list of location/time stops (tap one to focus it on the map).
   - A **map with the route line (polyline)** connecting all GPS points.
   - A side drawer to switch to another date.

## 10. Status Legend & Glossary

### Attendance / leave status colours
The app uses a colour legend ("All status") for attendance and leave states, including: **holiday**, **missing work / absent**, **incomplete time entry**, **late**, **early check-out**, and **normal**. Refer to the in-app legend for exact colours.

### Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **PIN** | Numeric code for quick re-entry into the app (set on first login). |
| **Branch / geofence** | Your check-in must match a branch location; coordinates can be corrected via *Change Location*. |
| **RP8** | The on-site field-visit collection report (includes GPS + photos), distinct from a phone-call result. |
| **Group ID** | A grouping code chosen when recording a call-collection result. |
| **Promise / payment date** | The date a customer commits to pay, recorded during collection. |
| **Lead** | A prospective sales customer to be contacted. |
| **Polyline / route** | The line drawn on the map connecting an employee's recorded GPS points. |
| **Coach system** | The external system that recorded check-in times are forwarded to. |
